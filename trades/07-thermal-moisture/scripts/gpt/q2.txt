## Basis and labels

- **[C]** = estimating convention, including waste allowances; not a manufacturer/FM requirement.
- **[S: name]** = input controlled by the named source and the selected roof assembly.
- Dimensions below are **feet**, areas **SF**, unless marked otherwise. Round purchased rolls, boards, bars, containers, and accessories up to whole units.
- Apply waste **once**, after including required laps and flashing material. Take off different thicknesses, widths, colors, and products separately.

## 1. TPO membrane SF, including laps and flashings

The most reliable cut-sheet takeoff is:

\[
A_{\text{field,cut}}=\sum_p(\text{cut width}_p)(\text{cut length}_p)
\]

Cut dimensions include the specified side laps and end laps.

For a rectangular roof section \(R\times D\), with \(n\) sheet courses and \(m\) segments per course:

\[
A_{\text{field,cut}}
=
[R+(n-1)o_s]\,[D+(m-1)o_e]
\]

where \(o_s,o_e\) are side- and end-lap widths. This includes overlap intersections.

Separate flashing strips:

\[
A_{\text{flashing}}
=
\sum_i d_i(L_i+J_i o_i)+\sum A_{\text{patches}}
\]

- \(d_i\): developed flashing width—vertical height plus required horizontal legs, turn-ins, and allowances.
- \(L_i\): installed flashing length.
- \(J_i\): number of flashing-strip joints.
- \(o_i\): overlap at each joint.
- Count continuous field-sheet turn-ups **either in the field cut sheet or as flashing, not both**.

\[
\boxed{A_{\text{membrane,order}}
=(A_{\text{field,cut}}+A_{\text{flashing}})(1+w_m)}
\]

**Laps, flashing dimensions, and patches:** **[S: selected manufacturer’s details, e.g., Carlisle Sure-Weld TPO installation instructions/details].**  
**Small penetrations:** commonly not deducted from field area **[C]**.

## 2. Membrane rolls

Nominal roll areas:

\[
10'\times100'=1{,}000\ \text{SF}
\]

\[
12'4''\times100'=\frac{37}{3}\times100
=1{,}233\tfrac13\ \text{SF}
\]

Area-based purchase allowances:

\[
\boxed{N_{10}=\left\lceil\frac{A_{\text{membrane,order}}}{1{,}000}\right\rceil}
\]

\[
\boxed{N_{12'4''}=\left\lceil
\frac{A_{\text{membrane,order}}}{1{,}233\tfrac13}
\right\rceil}
\]

**[C]** These are area-based counts; final counts require a roll-cutting layout because remnants may not furnish the required sheet lengths or flashing widths.

For preliminary rectangular course layout using roll width \(b\):

\[
n=\max\!\left(1,\left\lceil\frac{R-o_s}{b-o_s}\right\rceil\right)
\]

\[
m=\max\!\left(1,\left\lceil\frac{D-o_e}{100-o_e}\right\rceil\right)
\]

Do **not** deduct lap coverage from nominal roll area again when laps are already included in membrane-order SF.

## 3. Polyiso insulation: 4 × 8 boards per layer

For each layer \(\ell\):

\[
\boxed{N_{\ell}
=\left\lceil\frac{A_{\ell}(1+w_{\ell})}{32}\right\rceil}
\]

\[
N_{\text{total}}=\sum_{\ell}N_{\ell}
\]

Count separately by thickness, layer, and product. Staggering does not itself add another layer; associated cutting is included in waste **[C]**. Final board layout governs.

## 4. Fasteners and plates by FM zone

Use **nonoverlapping** field, perimeter, and corner areas:

\[
A_F+A_P+A_C=A_{\text{roof}}
\]

**Zone geometry and fastening requirements:** **[S: FM Property Loss Prevention Data Sheet 1-29 and the applicable FM RoofNav-approved assembly/project attachment schedule].**

### Insulation/cover-board attachment

For zone \(z\in\{F,P,C\}\):

\[
\boxed{F_{\text{ins},z}=\sum_k\sum_j B_{k,z,j}\,p_{k,z,j}}
\]

- \(k\): each independently required fastening pass.
- \(B_{k,z,j}\): **installed** boards/pieces receiving pattern \(j\), not purchased boards including waste.
- \(p_{k,z,j}\): specified fasteners per board/piece.

Where one plate is required per fastener:

\[
P_{\text{ins},z}=F_{\text{ins},z}
\]

If an approved schedule instead specifies fastener density \(\rho_z\), preliminary counts are:

\[
F_{\text{ins},z}\approx\left\lceil A_z\rho_z\right\rceil
\]

Final counts follow the required board/row layout. Do not multiply by insulation-layer count when a single fastening pass secures the complete stack.

### Mechanically attached membrane

For each attachment row segment \(r\), length \(L_{r,z}\), spacing \(s_{r,z}\) in inches:

\[
\boxed{F_{\text{mem},z}
=\sum_r\left[
\left\lceil\frac{12L_{r,z}}{s_{r,z}}\right\rceil+1
\right]}
\]

This expression assumes anchors at both segment ends; count shared end anchors once and follow specified end offsets.

\[
P_{\text{mem},z}=F_{\text{mem},z}
\]

when the specified system uses one plate per fastener. Include additional boundary/detail rows required by the assembly. Fully adhered membrane has no membrane field-attachment rows, but may have separate perimeter/detail securement.

\[
F_{\text{order}}
=\left\lceil
\left[\sum_z(F_{\text{ins},z}+F_{\text{mem},z})+F_{\text{details}}\right]
(1+w_f)
\right\rceil
\]

Calculate plates similarly, excluding fasteners that do not use plates.

**There is no universal FM field/perimeter/corner fastener count or multiplier.**

## 5. Membrane bonding adhesive gallons

If published coverage \(C_{\text{finished}}\) is **SF of completed bonded roof per gallon**, including all required coated faces:

\[
\boxed{G=\frac{A_{\text{bonded}}}{C_{\text{finished}}}(1+w_a)}
\]

If published coverage \(C_{\text{face}}\) is **SF per gallon per coated face**:

\[
\boxed{G=
\frac{\sum A_{\text{coated faces}}}{C_{\text{face}}}(1+w_a)}
\]

For equal coverage on two faces, the numerator is \(2A_{\text{bonded}}\). Do not double it when the published coverage already represents finished bonded area.

\[
N_{\text{containers}}=\left\lceil\frac{G}{G_{\text{container}}}\right\rceil
\]

Calculate field membrane and flashings separately if products or coverage differ.

**Coverage and number of coated faces:** **[S: selected adhesive product data sheet, e.g., Carlisle Sure-Weld Bonding Adhesive product data].**

## 6. Seam tape and cover strip LF

**Ordinary heat-welded TPO seams:**

\[
\boxed{L_{\text{seam tape}}=0}
\]

unless the specified system/detail expressly requires tape.

For specified cover-strip or tape runs:

\[
\boxed{L_{\text{order}}
=\left(\sum L_{\text{installed runs}}+\sum J_i o_i\right)(1+w_s)}
\]

\[
N_{\text{rolls}}=\left\lceil\frac{L_{\text{order}}}{L_{\text{roll}}}\right\rceil
\]

Take off each width/product separately; count discrete patches separately.  
**Required locations, widths, and overlaps:** **[S: selected TPO manufacturer’s details].**

## 7. Termination bar LF

\[
\boxed{L_{\text{bar,order}}=\sum L_{\text{actual bar locations}}(1+w_b)}
\]

\[
N_{\text{bars}}\approx
\left\lceil\frac{L_{\text{bar,order}}}{L_{\text{stock bar}}}\right\rceil
\]

Final count follows the cut schedule. Do not equate all roof perimeter LF with termination-bar LF.

For termination-bar fasteners, use the row-count formula above with the specified spacing and endpoint rules; these generally do not consume roof insulation plates.

**Locations and spacing:** **[S: selected manufacturer’s termination details/project details].**

## 8. Pipe boots, drains, and walkway pads

### Pipe boots

\[
\boxed{N_{\text{boots},d}
=\text{count of penetrations requiring boot type/size }d}
\]

Separate standard, split/retrofit, and field-fabricated flashing conditions.

### Drains

\[
\boxed{N_{\text{primary}}=\text{scheduled primary drain locations}}
\]

\[
\boxed{N_{\text{overflow}}=\text{scheduled overflow drain locations}}
\]

Separate new drains, retrofit inserts, and existing drains requiring flashing only. Count accessory components separately only when not included in the drain assembly.

### Walkway pads

Area-based allowance:

\[
\boxed{N_{\text{pads}}\approx
\left\lceil\frac{A_{\text{actual pad coverage}}(1+w_w)}
{A_{\text{one pad}}}\right\rceil}
\]

Exclude intentional gaps from actual pad coverage. Final count follows the pad layout.

For roll walkway:

\[
L_{\text{walkway,order}}=\sum L_{\text{strip runs}}(1+w_w)
\]

**Locations/counts:** project drawings. **Accessory compatibility and walkway gaps:** **[S: selected TPO manufacturer’s details].**

## 9. Cover board

For board area \(a_c\):

\[
\boxed{N_{\text{cover},\ell}
=\left\lceil\frac{A_{\text{cover},\ell}(1+w_c)}{a_c}\right\rceil}
\]

For 4 × 8 boards, \(a_c=32\) SF. Count each layer separately; include its attachment in Section 4 without duplicating shared fastening passes.

## 10. Tapered insulation crickets: SF, volume, board-feet

### Footprint SF

\[
A_{\text{rectangle}}=LW
\qquad
A_{\text{triangle}}=\frac{bh}{2}
\qquad
A_{\text{diamond}}=\frac{d_1d_2}{2}
\]

Split irregular crickets into triangles or rectangles.

### Thickness from slope

Let \(q\) be taper in **inches per foot**, and \(R\) the horizontal run in that taper direction:

\[
\boxed{\Delta t_{\text{in}}=qR}
\]

For thickness-gradient slope \(s\) in ft/ft:

\[
\Delta t_{\text{in}}=12sR
\]

Use the insulation’s thickness gradient relative to its substrate—not automatically the finished roof’s absolute slope.

### Exact volume using planar triangular facets

For each triangular facet \(j\), with vertex thicknesses \(t_1,t_2,t_3\) in inches:

\[
\bar t_j=\frac{t_1+t_2+t_3}{3}
\]

\[
\boxed{V_{\text{ft}^3}=\sum_j\frac{A_j\bar t_j}{12}}
\]

\[
\boxed{BF=\sum_j A_j\bar t_j=12V_{\text{ft}^3}}
\]

Useful special cases:

- Rectangular wedge from \(t_0\) to \(t_0+\Delta t\):
  \[
  \bar t=t_0+\frac{\Delta t}{2}
  \]
- Triangular facet with one high vertex and two vertices at \(t_0\):
  \[
  \bar t=t_0+\frac{\Delta t}{3}
  \]

\[
A_{\text{taper,allowance}}=A_{\text{footprint}}(1+w_t)
\qquad
BF_{\text{allowance}}=BF(1+w_t)
\]

Do not include uniform base layers already counted elsewhere. Final tapered-panel quantities must be counted by taper designation, thickness, and fill layer from the shop layout—not inferred from volume alone **[C]**.

## Typical estimating waste allowances

**All percentages below are [C], not requirements from the named sources.**

| Quantity | Typical waste allowance |
|---|---:|
| TPO membrane, after explicit laps/flashings | 5–10% simple; 10–15% cut-up/congested |
| Flat polyiso, each layer | 3–5% simple; 5–10% cut-up |
| Fasteners and plates | 3–5% |
| Bonding adhesive | 5–10%, beyond stated coverage consumption |
| Specified seam tape/cover strip | 5–10%, after explicit splices |
| Termination bar | 3–5% simple; 5–10% numerous short runs |
| Pipe boots | 0%; add explicitly requested spares |
| Drains | 0%; count scheduled units |
| Walkway pads/roll walkway | 5–10% for cutting; whole-pad layouts may need 0% |
| Cover board | 3–5% simple; 5–10% cut-up |
| Tapered cricket material | 5–10% simple; 10–15% complex; reconcile with panel layout |
