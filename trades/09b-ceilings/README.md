## Open questions (RFI, not silent changes)

- **Stud size at the cloud cove and the beam wrap.** A-630 detail 4 (cloud light cove, part of the 30.5 FT
  "SOFFIT @ 4'6"H, GWB @ 3'H" row) and A-131 detail 1 (steel-beam wrap at the ACT-2 edge) call **6"** metal studs; the
  estimate carries all ceiling and soffit framing (lines 284, 291) as 3-5/8". The LF rule is unchanged, but the size split is open.

- Not carried but specified or detailed: 10% ACT attic stock, ACT grid components and hangers, J-bead at gyp soffits.
