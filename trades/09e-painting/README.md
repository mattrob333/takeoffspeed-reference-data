## Scope

- **Interior wall paint by tag.** PT-1 is the field colour on every wall "U.N.O.". The accents are PT-4 (tech wall and phone room), PT-6 (the tech-wall recess, flat black), PT-7 (a clear wallcovering primer on the meeting room feature wall) and PT-8 (the meeting room window wall).
- **Ceiling paint.** PT-3 flat goes on the gypsum ceilings and on the vertical gypsum faces of the soffits. Exposed structure gets PT-3 at plan area in Janitor 115 and Elec/IT 106. PT-5 goes on top of the plywood booth roof.
- **Doors and frames.** PT-2 semi-gloss goes on the six hollow metal frames. The leaves are solid-core wood (factory finished, door trade). Storefront and the Type F slider are aluminium and are not painted.
- **Opening trim.** PT-2 on 910 LF of assumed interior trim (line 88).
- **MEP paint.** Black linear diffusers in the upper (open) ceiling, plus a flat-black LSR plenum. Roof-level gas pipe in safety yellow.
- **Not in these lines** (they belong to other trade folders): the CMU cementitious coating EPT-1 (line 673, A-190); bollards "PAINTED YELLOW" (lines 674, 713, site); the dumpster gate (line 678); pavement markings (645, 646); the wallcovering itself (lines 239 and 240, folder 09d); and painted wood trim from millwork. None of the lines carries paint **gallons**. Every quantity here is a net surface, count or length.

## Open points and findings

- **Line 304 / 88: 910 LF of opening trim is an assumption.** No drawing shows paint-grade trim at openings. The interior frames are HM, the glazing is aluminium storefront, and the Dirtt slider is prefinished. The best back-fit is 2 faces × (Σ door 2H+W 182.33 + EA-window perimeters 268.58) = 901.83, rounded up to 10 = 910. It counts exterior faces and leaves out storefront windows B, F, J and K, so it remains a plausible fit and not a proven source (GPT-6 Astra agrees). For the related sealant line 149 (1330 LF), the same opening set gives 910 + Σ all window perimeters 419.06 → 1330 after rounding up to 10 (GPT-6 Astra's reconstruction, also unproven).
- **PT-6 is double counted inside PT-4.** The 10.6 LF × 10' PT-4 tech-wall run includes the 37.2 SF recess that line 297 carries as PT-6. That is about 24% of line 296. The recess returns ("PAINT RECESS RETURNS ALL SIDES, PT-4") are partly in the run: the 10.6 FT run is about 0.8 ft longer than the 9'-9 3/4" (9.81 ft) finished wall width on A-704 detail 4, and on the p08 raster it visibly steps around the recess (left pier front, return, recess back, return, right pier front; pixel trace 10.59 ft; GPT-6 Astra traces the same path). So the two side returns are carried at the shallow depth drawn on A-150 (about 0.39 ft, against 9 1/2" on A-704) and at the full 10 ft height. The head and sill returns (2 × 7'-9 3/4" × 9 1/2" = 12.4 SF) cannot come from a plan run and are not carried.
- **PT-7 may be short.** The Gathering Area 103 north wall carries a `PT-4 / PT-7` tag. Only the Meeting Room 105 wall (10.3 FT) was measured for PT-7, so roughly 10.6 × 10 = 106 SF of primer may be missing.
- **Coat count.** PT-7 is a primer only (A-170, 09 9110 "N/A" finish). PT-7's line nonetheless says "one coat of primer with two coats of paint". The HM frames are shop-primed (08 1113 p214), and 09 9100 3.3.B.2 lets the field primer be touch-up only, but 09 9110 p328 still lists a primer system for HM frames and p320 says field priming is in addition to shop priming, so the frames' 1P+2C is a defensible convention, not an error.
- **Sheen and tag conflicts.** PT-5 is semi-gloss on the schedule and eggshell in line 302. PT-6 is eggshell on the schedule and "FLAT BLACK" on A-704. A-130 note E says gypsum ceilings are PT-1, while the RCP tags, the A-170 `GWB-1 PAINTED GYP. … PT-3` row and the line say PT-3; the schedule supports the estimator (an RFI is still worth logging).
- **Exposed structure is at plan area.** 1-1/2" Type B deck plus joists carry more surface than the plan area, and the spec states no factor. The engine warns about exactly this.
