## Material types and specified properties

- **Conduit** (26 1000 §2.1, §3.1, §3.3): RGS/IMC threaded, galvanized; EMT for
  branch circuits not underground; Schedule 40 PVC for underground feeders and
  service when encased in 4" concrete or under the slab. Exterior underground
  conduit encased in 4" of 3000 psi concrete, 30"–48" cover, 6" yellow 4-mil
  marker tape over the full length. Branch minimum 1/2", home runs minimum 3/4".
  Empty / telecom conduit gets a pull line (E-010 sheet note 7, 26 1000 §3.1 I).
  The transaction-bar bundles are called **IMC** on E-100 keynote 8.
- **Conductors** (§2.3, material and insulation): copper, 600 V; #10 and smaller
  solid THHN/THWN; #8 and larger stranded THW/XHHW. Sizing is in the installation
  part (§3.4 F Wiring, manual page 460): branch circuits not smaller than #12, and
  #10 minimum from the home-run outlet to the panel where the home run exceeds 100 ft.
  Riser (E-800): service 2 sets 4-#3/0 in 4"C, 400A feeder 2 sets 4-#3/0 + #3G in
  4"C, 225A feeder 4-#4/0 + #4G in 3"C. None of these is a separate estimate
  line; they sit in the allowance (line 510).
- **Boxes** (§2.4, §3.5): pressed steel 4" square / sectional boxes; cast boxes
  outdoors; covers on every junction box.
- **Disconnects** (§2.5): heavy-duty, quick-make, padlockable; NEMA 1 indoors,
  NEMA 3R outdoors; fused with dual-element current-limiting fuses.
- **Wall switches** (§2.7): 20A 277V, Hubbell CS1221 series. **Receptacles**
  (§2.9): Hubbell 5362 / CR20TR / GF20 series; plans call 20A 125V throughout.
  Conflict: §2.9 A says duplex receptacles are "125 volts and 15A rating" while
  naming 20A-grade models (5362, CR20, GF20); §2.9 B gives singles at 20A. The plans
  and every estimate receptacle line (534–552) read 20A 125V. Carry 20A and flag
  the spec conflict; do not resolve it silently.
- **Plates** (§2.10): brushed stainless; exterior self-closing die-cast WP8M.
  Plan overrides: black plates at the transaction bar and booths (E-100 notes),
  brown at the library wall (keynote 23), engraved "GFCI PROTECTED" (keynote 24).
- **Luminaires** (E-600, 26 3000): 22 interior types, LED, 120 V. Emergency
  variants carry an integral 90-minute battery (AE, FEM, GEM, JE, TE, X1–X3).
  Types C and UC carry "PROVIDE TEN (10) EXTRA FIXTURES FOR ATTIC STOCK".
- **Site lights** (E-011 schedule): 1P4F = 1 head, 2P4DA = 2 heads at 180°,
  both on 4SQB5 square poles, 27'-6".
- **Panels** (E-610, E-800): PP 208Y/120 3φ 400A MCB; RP-A 208Y/120 3φ 225A MLO;
  copper bus, bolt-on breakers, door-in-door trim.

## What the estimator measured

All 108 lines come from PlanSwift legends on 13 markup pages, except one
allowance. The estimator:

- **counted** every device, fixture, box, panel and room item with a dot per
  symbol (96 lines, EA);
- **measured** 9 linear items: 6 conduit routes and 3 linear fixture types (LF);
- carried **1 allowance** (line 510, 1 LS, basis "AREA: 2000 SF");
- typed **2 quantities by hand** where the PlanSwift row prints `NAN EA`
  (lines 497 and 541).

Conduit LF is the **route length of the bundle**. "(2) 4" conduit 101.71 LF" is
101.71 LF of trench route carrying two conduits, not 203.42 LF of conduit.

A legend count becomes a line one-to-one, or as the sum of the same item name on
several pages (plan + enlarged plan + elevation + mechanical sheet). The same
device shown on two pages is not added twice (page 87 repeats page 42; "LCP" on
page 49 repeats the LCP on page 46).

## Reconciliation

`deltaPct` in [`reconciliation.csv`](reconciliation.csv) is
`(recomputedQty − estimatorQty) / estimatorQty × 100`, the same convention as the
other trade folders. Positive means the markup rebuild is above the estimate
