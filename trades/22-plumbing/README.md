  hubless cast iron above.
- **Conflicts** (record them, do not resolve them silently):
  - *Water.* The P-101 "PIPE MATERIALS" table is primary/fallback. Primary: PEX-A above and below
    ground, Sch 40 PVC for waste, vent and condensate, cast iron for vent over 2". Its note: where
    those are not permitted by local code, use Type L copper (above-ground water), Type K copper
    (below-ground water), Type M copper (waste and vent under 2"), cast iron (waste 2" and larger)
    and Sch 40 wrought iron (condensate). The P-101 piping schedule and spec 22 1000 say Type L
    hard copper above ground (Type K below). Open item: PEX-A vs copper.
  - *Sanitary/vent above ground.* The P-101 piping schedule lists three sanitary materials: hubless
    cast iron (hangers 5'/15', notes 1, 2), DWV hard copper (10'/10'; 6'/10' at 1-1/4"), and
    "ALL / PVC SHED 40 DWV (sic) / solvent weld / DWV socket" (4'/10', note 3 "pipe material may be
    used where permitted by authority having jurisdiction", note 4). General note K: no PVC in a
    return-air plenum. Spec 22 1000 2.1.B says hubless cast iron above ground. Open item: PVC vs
    cast iron above ground, not only copper vs PEX.
  - *Floor drain strainer.* Spec 22 1000 2.1.G: JR Smith 2000 Series with 6" Type B **square**
    adjustable strainer, satin nickel bronze. P-101 FL: JR Smith #2010 with 6" **round** nickel
    bronze grate. #2010 is a 2000-series drain, so the series agree; the grate shape differs.

## What the estimator measured

The estimator used PlanSwift. Each pipe size and system is a linear legend item in **FT**. Each tag
is a count item in **EA**, measured on the sheet where the item is drawn. Every estimate quantity is
one legend value, or the sum of the same-named legend rows over P-301/P-302/P-303. Nothing is
derived, wasted or rounded:

- Pipe LF = Σ legend FT for that system and size over all sheets. The estimate keeps PlanSwift's
  2-decimal precision; the legend prints one decimal. Every LF line is within ±0.05 LF per legend row
  (`toleranceLF` in `fixtures.json`; `tolerancePct` is the same rule as a percentage of the expected
  quantity, minimum 0.5%, see formulas.md F1).
- Legend completeness: `legends.json` flags the P-402 (p59) and P-403 (p60) legend boxes as possibly
  clipped at the page edge. Re-rendered, neither box shows a bottom border: every markup page is a
  raster that stops 24 pt above the sheet bottom, so the side borders just end. The visible rows are
  still taken as complete, because every coloured mark on those pages has a visible legend row (p59:
  the FCO and NFWH dots; p60: the meter dot; found by a pixel scan) and no Div 22 estimate line is
  left without a source. Lines 429, 436 and 444 depend on these two legends.
- Vertical drops are drawn as straight off-plan segments in the run's colour (P-302: green 4" storm
  and purple HW lines extending above the plan from each "DN"/"UP TO RD/OD" point). They are already
  inside the legend totals. No separate riser takeoff exists.
- Fixtures, drains and devices: one count per tag, each counted on **one** sheet only (P-302 for
  fixtures, even though the same tags repeat on P-301 and P-402).
- Faucet sub-rows of the fixture schedule (LAV battery faucet, MS wall faucet, lounge/library faucets)
  are folded into the fixture's line; they have no count of their own.
