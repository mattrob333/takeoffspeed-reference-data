**Basis:** Count from approved plans, riser and sequence of operations—not floor area alone. References below use **NFPA 72-2019, NEC-2020 and IBC-2021**; adopted editions/local amendments govern. **† = estimating allowance, not a code minimum.**

## A. Addressable fire alarm

| Material | Counting rule |
|---|---|
| FACP/enclosure | **1/system** assumed; include required SLC/NAC/interface cards, avoiding duplicates included with panel |
| Annunciator; communicator | **1 each where specified**; communicator may be integral |
| Smoke/heat detectors | **1 head + 1 compatible base/location**; substitute sounder/isolator base where shown |
| Duct detector | **1 assembly/location**, sampling tube sized to duct; add remote test/indicator and shutdown interface where required |
| Manual stations | **1/shown location**; do not assume every exit requires one without checking applicable requirements |
| Horns, strobes, horn/strobes | **1 appliance/location**; combination horn/strobe = **1 appliance**, not 2 |
| Speakers/speaker-strobes | **1/location** if voice evacuation specified; include amplifiers/power equipment |
| Monitor modules | Count **independent monitored inputs**, then divide by usable inputs/module; waterflow and valve tamper normally remain separate points |
| Control/relay modules | Count **independently controlled functions/circuits**: HVAC shutdown, door release, etc.; distinguish supervised outputs from dry-contact relays |
| SLC isolators | Count engineered isolation boundaries; **no universal “1 per X devices” rule** |
| NAC boosters/remote supplies | Quantity from output count, load, voltage drop and distribution—not a fixed devices/supply ratio |
| Boxes/backboxes | **1 per required mounting enclosure**; account for shared boxes and integral enclosures. Combination appliance generally uses **1 box** |
| Accessories | Count EOL components **per supervised circuit requiring them**; mounting plates, covers, guards and labels per applicable location |

**Cable schedule—examples only; listing/manufacturer and voltage drop govern:**

- SLC: **1 × 18/2** cable along the actual loop/branch route; shielding as specified.
- NAC: **1 × 14/2 or 12/2** per circuit route.
- Auxiliary power: separate **2-conductor** cable where required; gauge calculated.
- Voice: typically **1 × shielded 18/2** per speaker-circuit route, if compatible with the equipment.
- Select **FPL/FPLR/FPLP** or permitted substitutes by installation environment—not simply “all low voltage.”
- Include required primary-power branch circuit(s), disconnect identification and monitoring; assign the electrical-package scope explicitly.

**Batteries:** Count **1 set per independently battery-backed source**. A typical 24 V source uses **2 × 12 V batteries in series**; Ah capacity does **not** double.

\[
Ah_{\text{base}}=24I_{\text{standby}}+\frac{5}{60}I_{\text{alarm}}
\]

For emergency voice/alarm service, use **15/60 h**, rather than 5/60 h, at the applicable alarm load. Apply the adopted-edition safety/aging factor and manufacturer requirements; round up to an approved battery size. Verify charger capacity and cabinet space. These durations are the usual battery-only protected-premises basis, not every permitted power arrangement.

## B. Access control—per single-leaf, entry-controlled door

Assume **1 entry reader and mechanical free egress**.

| Item | Quantity/door | Typical cable/run |
|---|---:|---|
| Reader | **1**; add **1** for controlled exit | **1 reader run**: OSDP—1 impedance-controlled RS-485 twisted pair plus 2 power conductors; Wiegand—typically **22/6 or 22/8**, manufacturer-specified |
| Electric strike/electrified lock | **1** | **1 × 18/2 or 16/2**; additional conductors for monitoring/functions |
| Door-position contact | **1/monitored leaf** | **1 × 22/2** |
| Powered REX sensor | **0 or 1** | If used, typically **1 × 22/4** |
| Passive REX switch | **0 or 1**, often integral to hardware | Typically **1 × 22/2**; do not automatically add a separate sensor |
| Power transfer/door loop | **1 only for powered leaf-mounted hardware** | Conductors matched to hardware; not normally needed for a frame-mounted strike |
| Reader/device/junction boxes | Per actual mounting detail | Do not assign one generic box to every concealed component |

System-level quantities:

- **Controllers:** \(\lceil D/c\rceil\), where \(D\) = controlled doors and \(c\) = usable doors/controller; also verify reader and I/O capacities and geographic grouping.
- **Power supplies:** count required power zones after checking simultaneous load, output count, voltage drop and release arrangement. **Not automatically 1/door.**
- **Backup batteries:** only as specified/required; access control has **no blanket NFPA 72 24-hour standby requirement**.
- **Network:** generally **1 Cat 6 home run/networked controller**, not necessarily 1/door.
- **Sensor-release locking:** include **1 independent manual unlocking device/door** and required fire-alarm/sprinkler release interfaces. Hardware-release arrangements differ. **Do not require FA release for every electric strike merely because it is access-controlled.**
- Count shared FA release relays by **approved release circuit/group**, and count them in **one trade only**.

## C. IP video surveillance

- **Cameras:** **1/location**; add **1 required mount**, enclosure/backbox and environmental accessories per mounting detail.
- **Horizontal cable:** **1 Cat 6 home run/camera** for typical Ethernet/PoE cameras.
- **PoE ports:** **1/camera**; planning allowance **\(\lceil1.20N\rceil\)†**. Count uplinks separately. Verify per-port PoE class and total **PSE power allocation**, including IR/heaters—not just port count.
- **NVR/VMS:** normally **1 appropriately sized recording system**; verify camera channels, incoming bitrate, recording throughput and **1 licence/camera where separately licensed**.
- **Storage:** use aggregate average recording bitrate—not camera count alone:

\[
\boxed{\text{Usable TB}=0.0108\times N\times b_{\text{Mbps}}\times d_{\text{days}}\times f_{\text{recording}}}
\]

Example: **8 cameras × 4 Mbps × 30 days × continuous recording = 10.37 TB usable**, before overhead/reserve. A **20%†** allowance gives **12.44 TB usable**. Motion-recording duty factor is **uncertain unless substantiated**.

- Count actual HDDs after RAID, hot-spare and formatting allowances; **raw disk TB ≠ usable recording TB**.
- Count UPS units by designated protected equipment group, **if specified**.

## LF and termination conventions—all three systems

1. **Measure actual routed centerline:** horizontal route + vertical rises/drops + offsets. Single-storey does not mean zero vertical LF.
2. **FA daisy chain:** panel-to-first-device home run + every device-to-device segment. Add required return legs for Class A; measure required separated routing. Do **not** home-run every addressable device unless designed that way.
3. **Access/video:** count each actual home-run or distributed-controller segment. A composite cable is **1 cable run**, not several separately purchased cables.
4. **Slack allowance:** **2 ft/field cable termination + 10 ft/panel or rack termination†**. Count incoming/outgoing cable ends separately. Add specified service loops separately.
5. **Cable LF:** route + slack. Keep optional **5% ordering waste†** separate from installed LF. A 100-ft run of **18/2 = 100 cable-ft**, not 200 cable-ft.
6. **Conduit LF:** count shared raceway **once**, regardless of cable count. Count fittings, boxes, supports, sleeves and firestopping from actual routing/penetrations.
7. **Ethernet:** normally **90 m/295 ft permanent link; 100 m/328 ft channel**, including applicable cords; slack consumes the allowance. Check temperature/cord-related derating.
8. **Typical jack-to-jack camera link:** **1 patch-panel port + 1 field jack + 2 patch cords**. Approved MPTL/direct-camera termination instead uses **1 field plug + 1 rack patch cord**; do not count both arrangements.
