## 2. Reading each input

* **Cabinet LF:** read the dimension chain under (bases) or over (uppers) the elevation. Group boxes by the section bubble that
  cuts them — the bubble number *is* the cabinet type (A-701 rows "5", "7", "9" in the legend are exactly the section numbers). Add a
  dimensioned 1" filler that sits between boxes in the same run (A-707 elev 3 `1'-5"` + `1"` = 1.5 LF); do not add 1½" end panels.
* **Depth and height** come from the section, not the elevation: A-703 sections print `1'-10 3/4"` (estimate writes 1'10"/1'11"),
  A-707 sections `1'-8 3/4"` box + 2" toe = 2'-0" nominal. Heights: base boxes to underside of top (`2'-8"`, `2'-10"`, `3'-4"`/`3'-6"`),
  uppers `3'-0"`/`2'-6"`.
* **Top SF:** overall plan length × depth; add stub rectangles (A-707 plan 1: `1 3/4"` + `7 5/8"` wide × `10 1/4"` deep step).
  Library depth `1'-0"`; phone room top spans the room wall to wall at 2'-0" (no printed width; ~5'-4" scaled on A-708 det. 10,
  ~5'-5" on A-120; legend 5.5 ft). On A-708 det. 10 the `3'-9 7/8"` string is the wall-to-pedestal (MS-03) clearance, not the top.
* **Waterfall faces (SLD-1):** every slate-blue elevation face on A-700 (side elevation 5, elevation 6, back elevation 3 end block,
  1¼" edges on front elevation 4) is part of the SLD-1 SF line.
* **Splash LF:** only edges against a wall that the section shows with a splash ("PLAM COUNTERTOP w/ EASED EDGES & BACKSPLASH").
  Find the back wall from the elevation marker: A-707 plan 1 is viewed by elevation 3 from above, so its back wall is the *bottom*
  edge (`6'-4 1/8"` − `7 5/8"` = `5'-8 1/2"`, then the notch `1'-1 3/4"` + `7 5/8"` and the `10 1/4"` stub end); the `6'-4 1/8"` edge is
  the open front. Plan 2's back wall is its top edge (`5'-4"`).
* **Band heights:** read the stacked string, not the overall. A-700 back elevation 3 prints `1 1/4"` (SLD-1 edge) / `6 1/4"` (PL-3
  band) / `1 1/4"`; the separate `7 1/2"` is edge + band.
* **Laminate faces:** read W × H of each face with that tag. The PL-4 staff-side face of the transaction bar is not drawn as its own
  elevation; take the front tile field `(10'-6 3/4" − 2 × 1 1/4") × 3'-8 1/2"`.
* **Booth:** walls run in plan 1 from the storefront to the PNL-1 screens: outside faces `8'-0"` (plan 1 overall); inside faces
  `8'-0"` (section 5, cut looking at the inside face: `1'-11 1/2" 1/2" 4'-0" 1/2" 1'-11 1/2"`; plan 1 subdivides the last panel as
  `1'-7 1/2" 3" 1"`, i.e. 7'-8" to the PNL-1 pocket); height `9'-0" A.F.F.`, ends `9"`, header `9"` (elev 6) across `9" + 8'-6 1/2" + 9"`.
  Ceiling: `7'-6"` (RCP 11 `8'-0"` less the `6"` Mechoshade pocket: `1'-5 1/2" 1/2" 4'-0" 1/2" 1'-7 1/2" 3" 1"`) × (`1'-4"` + `4'-6 1/2"` +
  `2'-8"`). The `7'-6"` belongs to the ceiling only.
* **Counts:** grommets from the plan symbols and the printed "(3 TOTAL)"; seats/table/locker from A-704 plan 8.

## 4. Cross-checks

* Σ cabinet LF of one elevation = the overall dimension minus end panels/fillers (A-703: 5 × 1'-6" + 2'-2" = 9'-8" vs `9'-10"` overall
  with two 1" fillers).
* SLD-1: back counter and service counter legends equal length × depth (18.06, 19.67); the transaction bar legend is ~1.8× its plan
  area because it includes waterfalls.
* Same polygon on three trades: A-700 WT-3 tile = ¼" cementitious backer = PL-4 = 38.4 SF.
* A-707: PL-2 tops 1+2 by dims (22.46) = legend 22.6.
* Booth reveal LF ≈ 4 faces × 2 × 9' + 2 × ceiling length (89.08) = legend 89.3.

## 5. Traps hit on this job

1. **Wrong page scale on a mixed-scale sheet.** A-707 plans are ½"=1'-0", elevations ¾"=1'-0". The "Linear" 16.5 FT drawn through
   the uppers on the elevations reads 1.5× long; true value 11.04 LF (estimate 11.0). Calibrate per viewport.
2. **NTS views and over-traced polygons.** A-706 det. 10 BOOTH 3D VIEW (NTS) carries purple and red markups, and the A-706 section-5
   purple polygon runs past the booth over the title block (legend 296.9 vs 309.03 SF rebuilt); A-702 elev 2's salmon PL-1 polygon
   fills the cased opening and runs ~4 ft past the millwork (legend 95.6 vs 53.06 SF rebuilt); A-701 counts the plan footprint as PL-1.
   Legends can be inflated or deflated; rebuild from dimensions.
3. **Not-to-scale bay inside a dimensioned elevation.** A-702 elev 2's `3'-6"` bay is drawn ~2'-6" wide — scale from the string, not pixels.
4. **Unit error.** Line 144 reveal is LF but entered as SF.
5. **Wrong sheet reference.** Library lines 113–116 cite A701; the library is A-702. The transaction-bar upper cabinet (line 99) lives
   on A-702 det. 4, called from the A-700 plan.
6. **Legend rows without names** ("5", "7", "9", "Linear", "NO" = NAN EA). Map them through the section bubbles or the drawn line.
7. **Same colour, different item:** A-707 cyan = 4"H PL2 base *and* (2) 1x4 blocking; A-705 blue = WT9 tile and (3) 2x4 blocking.
8. **Owner/vendor scope** inside the millwork plans: TCR equipment, steel cabinets, pencil drawer (owner provides, millwork mounts),
   lockers (furniture vendor; also on furniture line 367 — double-count risk), PNL-1 booth screen (Big Apple Visual Group, line 336).
9. **Strings that look like the item but are not:** A-708 det. 10 `3'-9 7/8"` is wall-to-pedestal clearance (the top spans the room);
   A-700 `7 1/2"` is quartz edge + PL-3 band (the band is `6 1/4"`).
10. **Spec quantities in prose:** 12 3669 "1 grommet per every 4 feet of countertop" — a count that appears only in the spec.
11. **Not taken off:** A-702 det. 6 "CABINET @ BOOTH" (`4'-3 1/4"(VERIFY)` wall cabinet over the booth, shown on A-706 3D view) has no estimate line.
