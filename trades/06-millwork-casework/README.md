## What the estimator measured, and how

PlanSwift markups exist for A-700 (p23), A-701 (p24), A-702 (p25), A-703 (p26, p27), A-704 (p28), A-706 (p29), A-707 (p30), A-705 (p32),
A-110 (p03) and A-410 (p16). A-708 (p31) has **no legend** (two un-legended green lines on det. 12).

* **Cabinets: LF of box width on the elevation.** One line per cabinet type (depth × height × configuration) per room. Where a legend
  exists (BASE CABINET, UPPER CABINET, rows named only by section number "5", "7", "9"), the estimate carries the **unrounded**
  PlanSwift value (2.02, 5.66, 4.66). Where no legend exists (A-703, A-705, A-707) the estimate is the elevation dimension string
  (9'-8" → 9.66, 2'-2" → 2.17).
* **Countertops: SF of plan area** (PL-2, PL-1, SLD-1). The SLD-1 area on A-700 also includes the waterfall/vertical solid-surface faces
  traced on elevations 3–6 (73.4 SF for a ~41 SF plan top).
* **Backsplash: LF of wall edge** (4"H PL-2).
* **Shelves: LF = shelves × run** (counted in axonometrics where no legend).
* **Laminate finish faces: SF by finish tag** (PL-1, PL-3, PL-4, PL-5) traced on elevations (and, at A-701, on the plan footprint).
* **Booth panelling: SF** of wall faces and of the ceiling; reveal by **LF** (written as SF in the estimate — a unit error).
* **Built units and accessories: EA** (check desk, locker, table, seats, pencil drawer, grommets).
* **Rounding convention:** areas and some LF are rounded to a convenient figure — usually to the whole SF/LF and usually up
  (17.4→18, 13.6→14, 5.5→6, 296.9→300, 89.3→90, 6.6→7, 0.875→1.0, 5.3→5.5) but sometimes down (6.2→6, 83.1→83, 64.2→64).
