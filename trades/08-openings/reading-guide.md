## 2. Hardware: the door schedule decides which set applies to which door

- **A-601** lists each set as `Set N.0 / Doors: … / Description / items (qty, item, model, finish, mfr)`. Sets printed here are
  1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 and 14.
- **Set 5.0 (1900 Narrow) lists "Doors: 101 (Entry)"**, and so does set 4.0. The door schedule says 101 is HW 4, so set 5 is a
  prototype alternate. **Set 8.0** is for "(Storage Rooms) (1900N IT ROOM)", and no such door exists. Neither is carried.
- Abbreviations (A-600 general notes): OT = by others; MC = Medeco cylinder by Owner; DT = DIRTT; PE Pemko, SA Sargent, RO Rockwood,
  RF Rixson, NO Norton, BEA, SU Securitron, VD Von Duprin, HS HES, MK McKinney. "Credential reader by Div 28" and "seals… by aluminum door mfr."
  are listed in the sets but are not GC-furnished hardware.
- The estimate cites the hardware as "A0.0". That sheet does not exist; read A-601.

## 3. Windows and storefront: A-610 plus the tags on A-120

- **A-610** has ten elevations at 3/8"=1'-0": WINDOW A (det. 1) … WINDOW K (det. 10). Each one carries an overall width string on top,
  an overall height string at the side, the lite and mullion strings (2" exterior, 1 3/4" interior), and the door bay drawn with a dashed X.
- Overall sizes as printed: A 12'-11 1/2" × 12'-0"; B 9'-11" × 12'-0"; C 7'-4" × 12'-0"; D 8'-0" × 9'-4"; E 6'-0" × 7'-4"; F 6'-0" × 10'-0";
  G 13'-4" × 9'-4"; H 14'-0" × 9'-4"; J 7'-8 1/4" × 12'-0"; K 7'-7 5/8" × 10'-0".
- The door bays show which frames are storefront: **B** holds door 100 (6'-0" pair bay, 8'-0" below the transom bar), **F** holds door 110 (3'-0"),
  **J** holds door 101 (6'-0" between 1 3/4" mullions; 3'-10 1/4" top row and 1 3/4" transom bar, then door bay = 5'-2 1/8" + 1 3/4" + 2'-8 1/8" = 8'-0"), and **K** holds door 105 (3'-4" bay; 2'-8 1/2" top row, then door bay = 4'-2 1/4" + 1 3/4" + 2'-6 1/4" + 1 3/4" = 7'-0").
- **Tags**: hexagons with a letter. Count them on **A-120**, which shows A1, B1, C1, D1, E2, F1, G1, H1, J1, K1.
  **A-110 has no tag for window A**, even though the window is drawn at the entry next to B. A model that counts tags on A-110 alone misses line 208.
  Watch the text layer too: A-120 also prints the column-grid letters A–E in large bubbles. In the PDF text layer the tag letters are 9.5 pt and the grid-bubble letters 12.5 pt (ratio ~1.3; about 13 px and 17 px when rendered at 96 dpi).
- Elevation cross-check: A-200 shows B, C, D, E, H and A-201 shows E, F, G (E appears once on each). A, J and K do not appear on the elevations.
- Glazing notes on A-610 (upper right): the exterior BOD is YKK YES45 TU, 2" × 4 1/2", with a 1" Solarban 90 (2) Acuity IGU. The interior BOD is YKK YES45 FS,
  1 3/4" × 4 1/2", 1/4" clear fully tempered. Under K: "NOTE: DIRTT WALL SYSTEM BY DIRTT WALL VENDOR. FINISH TO MATCH EXTERIOR STOREFRONT FINISH."
- **Markup p22**: count dots on A, C, D, E (×2 on one elevation), G and H. Area fills: cyan on B and F (exterior), dusty rose on J (YES45 FS), bright green on K
  (DIRTT). Every fill leaves the door bay unshaded. A yellow "DETAIL 1.0 EA" dot sits on the 2/A-440 callout of window A. It is a bookmark, not an item.

## 4. Roof hatch (A-140, spec 07 7200)

- A-140 markup legend: `30"x36" ROOF ACCESS HATCH - 3/A450` gives 1.0 EA (cyan dot). Detail 3/A-450 and spec p.200 (Bilco S-20, 30 by 36 in.)
  confirm it. The ladder below it (A-110 legend "1'9"W ROOF ACCESS LADDER - 1/A312") is Div 05.

## 5. VAT frame (A-701 detail 6, A-110 keynote P3)

- A-701 det. 6 VAT ACCESS ELEVATION is at 3"=1'-0". The strings across the top are `1 1/2"` (cabinet edge to frame), `1"`, `8 1/2"`, `1"`; the vertical string is
  `1"`, `1'-0 3/4"`, `1"`. So the opening is 8 1/2" × 12 3/4", and the frame face is 1".
  Vector check on the sheet: frame outline 189.2 × 265.8 pt at 18 pt/in, which is 10.51" × 14.77".
- Note: "SINGLE SIDED VAT TUBE BY BANK VENDOR. G.C. FURNISH - PREFINISHED, MITERED BLACK ANGLED ALUMINUM VAT FRAME AROUND VAT TERMINAL OPENING."
  A-110 keynote P3 says "(BOTH SIDES)". The estimator followed A-701 and took one face.
- No PlanSwift row measures it, and det. 6 carries no coloured trace. The rows "5", "7" and "9" on p24 are millwork sections. The unnamed row
  `Linear 15.3 FT` (slate blue) was checked: it is the top and back of millwork section 5 traced at the page's 3/4" scale (7.4 ft true at 1 1/2"),
  not the VAT frame; 15.3 / 4 = 3.825 is coincidental.

## Cross-checks

- 10 door-schedule marks give 10 door EA across lines 190–196 (1 + 3 + 2 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1). They also give 10 hardware sets across lines 198–206 (8 × 1 + 2).
- The legend on p21 sums to the same counts (16 rows: 7 door rows = 10 EA, 9 hardware rows = 10 EA).
- 10 window types: 6 EA lines (7 units) plus 4 storefront frames in SF. Each A-610 elevation is used exactly once.
- Storefront doors (100, 101, 105, 110) sit inside storefront frames (B, J, K, F). Each door bay is deducted from exactly one SF line.

## Traps hit on this job

1. A-110 has no window-A tag, so count tags on A-120.
2. The schedule's width for a pair is the **pair** width (6'-0"). The estimate writes "(2) 3'Wx8'H".
3. Door 101 ENTRY is interior, because it is the inner vestibule door. 100 is the exterior pair.
4. Hardware set 5 lists door 101 as well. The door schedule governs.
5. The schedule's `xxx / H / 3'-0" × 1'-0 5/8"` row is an unfilled template, not a door.
6. Room numbers are not door numbers on A-110.
7. The spec storefront basis is Kawneer Trifab, but the drawings and estimate use YKK YES45. The spec also has a curtain-wall section (Kawneer 1600) that the drawings never use.
8. The roof hatch description in the estimate (11 ga thermally broken cover, 3" polyiso, SS 316 pintle hinge) differs from spec 07 7200 (14 ga stainless).
   The quantity is unaffected.
9. Legend names are truncated by PlanSwift ("…FS - …", "…@DIRT…") and keep the estimator's typo ("FIFTHHIRDSUPPORT"). Match legend rows by quantity,
   colour and position, not by text alone.
10. The keynote "BOTH SIDES" (A-110 P3) conflicts with the detail's "SINGLE SIDED" (A-701).
