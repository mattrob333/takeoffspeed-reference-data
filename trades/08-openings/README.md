EA line. A glazed frame with no door is a WINDOW counted EA by type tag.** No window or storefront area is carried in SF for A, C, D, E, G or H.

## Material types and their specified properties (as carried)

- **Type B / C doors**: 3'-0"×7'-0" solid-core wood (SCW), with welded HM frames (B) or a knock-down HM frame (C, door 111).
- **Type D**: medium-stile aluminum storefront pair, 2 × 3'-0"×8'-0" (scheduled as 6'-0"). **Type E**: medium-stile single, 3'-0"×8'-0".
- **Type F**: DIRTT thin-profile sliding glass door, 3'-4"×7'-0", 32.34" clear opening, purchased through DIRTT.
- **Exterior storefront / windows**: YKK YES45 TU, centre-set, thermally broken, 2"×4½" mullions, 1" IGU Solarban 90 (2) Acuity, VLT 53%, U 0.24, SHGC 0.23.
- **Interior storefront J**: YKK YES45 FS, centre-set, 1¾"×4½", ¼" fully tempered clear. **Interior K**: DIRTT wall system by the DIRTT vendor, finish to match the exterior.
- **Hardware**: Sargent L-lever locks, 8000/AD8400 exit devices, 281 closers, Norton 6061 operators, HES strikes, Pemko continuous hinges, McKinney TA2714/TA2314 butts, Rockwood trim. Items marked OT are by others, MC cylinders are by the owner, and DT items are by DIRTT.
