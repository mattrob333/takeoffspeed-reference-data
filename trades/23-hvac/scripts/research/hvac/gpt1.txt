### 2. Sheet-metal duct takeoff

- **Rectangular straight duct:** SF = `2(W + H)L`, dimensions in feet.
- **Round straight duct:** SF = `πDL`, dimensions in feet.
- **Sheet-metal weight:** lb = `Σ(SF by gauge × lb/SF)`.
- **Length:** centerline LF; separate straight sections and fittings without counting fitting lengths twice.
- **Elbows:** developed surface SF; constant-section elbows may use perimeter × centerline arc length.
- **Transitions:** developed panel SF using actual slant dimensions—not simply perimeter × axial length.
- **Fittings allowance:** **10–25% A** of straight-duct sheet area for preliminary takeoff only; **0% additional** when fittings are individually measured.
- **Sheet-metal waste:** **5–10% A** added to net developed sheet quantities.
- **Reinforcement, flanges, cleats, rods, angles:** separate LF/EA/lb; not included in skin weight.

**Source:** geometric measurement; SMACNA, *HVAC Duct Construction Standards—Metal and Flexible*, rectangular/round construction and fittings sections describe construction. **The percentages above are estimating assumptions, not published SMACNA/ASHRAE allowances.**

### 4. External wrap and internal liner

- **External fiberglass wrap, net SF:** exterior metal perimeter × LF, plus fitting surface SF.
- **Wrap purchase SF:** manufacturer’s stretch-out/cut length × roll width; include thickness-related stretch-out.
- **Facing lap:** **2 in typical ?**; avoid adding it again where supplied as a factory flange.
- **Compression:** **25%** for products whose installed R-values use that condition: installed thickness = `0.75 × nominal thickness`; **not 25% additional SF**.
- **Wrap waste:** **5–10% A** beyond required cuts/laps.
- **Fiberglass liner, approximate SF:** interior metal perimeter × LF, plus fittings; reconcile corner overlaps with panel cut sizes.
- **Clear-air dimensions:** rectangular metal width/height = clear width/height **+ 2 × liner thickness**.
- **Liner waste:** **10–15% A**; liner joints generally butt, rather than overlap.

**Sources:** Owens Corning, *SOFTR Duct Wrap FRK* product data, **Installation and installed R-value tables**; *QuietR Duct Liner* installation instructions, **cutting/fitting and joint requirements**. Product-specific lap/compression requirements govern; waste percentages are assumptions.

### 5. Sealant, tape and mastic

- **Sealant:** LF of required longitudinal seams + transverse joints + fitting seams.
- **Cartridges:** required bead LF ÷ manufacturer’s LF/cartridge.
- **Mastic:** coated SF ÷ manufacturer’s SF/gal at specified thickness.
- **Theoretical mastic gallons:** `coated SF × wet-film thickness in mils ÷ 1,604`.
- **Tape:** required joint LF × prescribed layers/wraps; rolls = `ceil(total LF ÷ usable LF/roll)`.
- **Waste:** **10% A** preliminary allowance.
- **Closure materials:** sheet-metal duct sealant/mastic; listed closure systems where required for fiberglass or flexible duct.

**Sources:** SMACNA, *HVAC Duct Construction Standards—Metal and Flexible*, **Table 1-2, Standard Duct Sealing Requirements**; selected closure manufacturer’s **coverage/application section**. **No universal LF/gal or LF/cartridge applies.**

### 6. Fire/smoke dampers and access

- **Fire damper:** **1 EA per required protected opening**.
- **Smoke damper:** **1 EA per required protected opening**.
- **Combination fire/smoke damper:** **1 EA assembly**, not two dampers.
- **Access:** **1 access provision per damper minimum takeoff convention**; additional access where needed for inspection/service.
- **Access doors:** count actual EA; an approved removable duct section may replace a separate door.
- **Actuators/end switches:** actual EA from damper submittals; not necessarily one per large multisection assembly.

**Source:** 2021 *IMC*, **§607.3, Damper Testing and Ratings; §607.4, Access and Identification; §607.5, Where Required**. Not every rated penetration automatically requires a damper.

### 8. Controls points

- **1 AI** per separately wired analog measurement.
- **1 AO** per separately wired modulating command.
- **1 DI/BI** per separately wired binary status/contact.
- **1 DO/BO** per separately wired binary command.
- **Temperature + humidity combination sensor:** typically **2 AI** when separately hardwired.
- **Modulating valve + end switch:** typically **1 AO + 1 DI**.
- **Network points:** count mapped objects separately from physical I/O.
- **Controllers, gateways, software points and spare I/O:** separate quantities; avoid counting network objects again as physical terminals.

**Source:** ANSI/ASHRAE Standard **135-2020, Clause 12, standard object types**; project controls points schedule. BACnet defines objects, **not mandatory equipment point counts**.
