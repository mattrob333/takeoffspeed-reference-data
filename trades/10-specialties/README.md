## Scope findings (not estimate lines; for the reviewer)

1. **36" rear grab bar missing: 2 EA.** Detail 2/A-410 prints the rear bar at 36" MIN, and elevation 9 (B) draws it behind
   the water closet (scales to ~36").
   ADA 604.5.2 requires a 36" min rear bar. GPT-6 Astra, asked independently, read the same three bars.
2. **Coat hooks are carried twice.** TA-09 (2 EA, line 327) points to the door hardware schedule. Hardware set 11.0,
   carried as line 205 with EA 2 (doors 112 and 113), lists "2 Coat Hook RM817" per door, which is 4 more. Convention in
   this folder: the hardware set carries the hooks and the TA-09 count is a cross-reference to flag, not a second
   quantity.
3. **Knox box (K.B.), 1 EA on G-001, is in no estimate line.** The M.C.# maximum-capacity sign is in the G-001 legend but
   placed nowhere on the plan, so no line is right for it.
4. **Extinguishers not carried, and a rating conflict.** Spec 10 44 13 refers the extinguishers to a separate section,
   10 44 16 (2 × 10 lb, one per F.E.C.). Line 335 reads only "FIRE EXTINGUISHER CABINET", and DIV 10 has no extinguisher
   line: unresolved scope (omitted, owner-furnished or bought elsewhere; the estimate does not say). The G-001 legend says
   4A:80B:C and spec 10 44 16 says 4-A:60-B:C.
5. **Room-ID signs.** G-002 note 25 says all signs identifying permanent rooms need raised characters and braille. The
   estimator carried only the restroom signs and the tactile exit signs (A-200 and A-201 put exterior signage under a
   separate signage package). Interior room-ID signage has no count on any sheet, so it is a scope question, not a missed
   count.

(A-706, A-410, A-110, A-120, A-601, G-001, G-002, spec pp. 337–339, 345), fresh renders (A-706 elevation 6, ceiling plan

A-706 callouts (Z-clip, plywood and stud build-up = PL-1 wall, not PNL-1). Every quantity was recomputed in Python.
Reconciliation, fixtures and estimate still agree line by line: **15 of 15 lines at 0.0 %** (14 counted, 1 measured).
No quantity changed.

What changed (all 13 issues upheld):

- **PNL-1** is now described as the Big Apple Visual Group double-sided patterned panel with hardwood blocking filler. The
  PL-1 laminate, Z-clips, plywood substrate and 16-ga studs are moved to the PL-1 booth wall (A-706 legend carries it as
  its own 296.9 SF row). Widths now cite the plan-11 dimensions 2'-8" and 1'-4", and the height cites elevation 6's
