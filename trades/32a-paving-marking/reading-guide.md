# Reading guide: 32a paving, marking, signs, bike racks, bollards

## 1. Sheets to open, in order

1. **C-3.0 Site Plan.** This is the takeoff sheet. Read these four blocks first:
   - **KEY NOTES** (right column, 23 numbered hexagon notes)
   - **PAVEMENT LEGEND** (two hatches, each with a geotech reference: "CONCRETE PAVEMENT (SEE GEOTECH REPORT BY TERRACON, DATED 05.04.2026)" with triangle stipple, "HEAVY DUTY PAVEMENT (SEE GEOTECH REPORT BY TERRACON, DATED 05.04.2026)" with a dot stipple; plus the note "*CONTRACTOR TO SUBMIT JOINT PLAN TO GEOTECH FOR REVIEW/APPROVAL PRIOR TO PAVING"). General Site Note 9 repeats the reference: "ALL NEW PAVEMENT SHALL MEET OR EXCEED GEOTECHNICAL SPECIFICATION. SEE REPORT PREPARED BY TERRACON, DATED 05.04.2026".
   - **SITE SUMMARY** ("PARKING PROV.: 11 SPACES", "STANDARD STALL DIMENSIONS: 9' x 18'", "MIN. DRIVE WIDTH: 24'", "IMPERVIOUS AREA: 21,033 S.F.")
   - **GENERAL SITE NOTES.** Note 5, "1 HANDICAP SPACE IS REQUIRED TO BE VAN ACCESSIBLE", sets the ADA counts. Note 1, "ALL DIMENSIONS ARE TO THE FACE OF CURB", sets the measuring line.
2. **C-6.0 Construction Details 1.** Holds every detail the C-3.0 keynotes point to (sidewalk, curb & gutter, stop bar, pavement marking, ADA parking, ADA symbol, ADA sign, sign mountings, bollard). The estimator highlighted the relevant titles and notes in purple on markup page 79.
3. **C-6.1.** GDOT standard ADA ramp and detectable warning (keynote 6).
4. **C-6.2.** Keynotes 3, 4, 18 and 21 send you here "FOR PAVEMENT SPECIFICATIONS". Open it to confirm the trap: it contains only sanitary sewer details. The Pavement Legend on C-3.0 sends you to the geotech report instead.
5. **Terracon geotechnical report (dated 05.04.2026), PDF pp. 31–32.** "Asphaltic Concrete (AC) Design" and "Portland Cement Concrete (PCC) Design" tables. The C-3.0 Pavement Legend references this report, so these are the design pavement sections for the job.
6. **A-190 Trash Enclosure.**
   - Wall section 3 gives the bollard (6" Ø, 5'-0", 16" pier). The pier is drawn with break lines and has **no depth dimension**; the 1'-4" and 1'-0" dimensions belong to the CMU wall footing.
   - General note: "ALL PAINTED STEEL PIPE BOLLARDS ARE TO RECEIVE A BOLLARD COVER".
   - Key notes TE1–TE5 describe the enclosure items.
   - Floor plan 4 shows the bollard locations.
7. **A-401 Bank Equipment.**
   - Enlarged drive-thru island plan: 8 bollard circles.
   - TYPICAL BOLLARD detail 8: 16" Ø concrete fill; pipe "48" BELOW GRADE" (top of paving to pipe bottom) plus 3" of fill below the pipe (51" total); 4'-0" exposed height.
   - NOTE block: bollard cover, 24" & 48" heights.
8. **Spec 05 5000** (pp. 118, 120) for bollard pipe and installation. **09 9110** (p. 329) for bollard paint. There is no Division 32 section (the TOC on p. 9 lists 32 3913 but it is not bound).

## 4. Cross-checks

1. **Impervious area.** Measured areas + building + 2' × curb LF = 20,810 SF, against the Site Summary's 21,033 SF (−1.1%). See formulas.md.
2. **Stall count.** The circled stall counts on C-3.0 read **7** (north row) and **5** (west row: the 11' van ADA stall plus four 9' stalls), which is 12. The Site Summary says "PARKING PROV.: 11 SPACES". The drawing contradicts itself. No line here depends on it, because the SSWL is measured, but a stall-row formula fed from the summary would come up one row-stall short.
3. **Keynote tag counts** against the legend counts (section 2).
4. **Enlarged plans against the site plan.** A-401 shows 8 bollards and the legend says 8, which agrees. A-190 shows **6** bollards but C-3.0 draws 5, which does not (see trap 1).
5. **Demolition as a sanity check.** The existing C&G removed was 987.2 LF; the new C&G is 993.9 LF. A similar footprint is expected.

## 5. Traps hit on this job

1. **Bollard count (civil vs architectural).** C-3.0 itself draws 3 circles on the enclosure back wall plus a gate post and a bollard at each jamb: 5 bollards, which is what the markup and estimate carry. On A-190 floor plan 4 there are 4 circles on the back wall (spaced 2'-4" / 3'-7" / 3'-9" / 3'-0" / 2'-0" = 14'-8") and 2 circles at each gate jamb; at each jamb the upper circle is the "GATE" post (TE2) and the lower is a "BOLLARD". That makes 6. Keynote 11 says "REFER TO ARCHITECTURAL PLANS", so A-190 governs and 6 is likely, pending RFI. GPT-6 Astra read both sheets the same way, independently.
2. **The keynotes point to the wrong sheet; the legend points to the right source.** Keynotes 3, 4, 18 and 21 reference C-6.2, which holds no pavement section. The C-3.0 Pavement Legend (and Site Note 9) reference the Terracon report dated 05.04.2026, which is the design section. The estimator ignored that reference and wrote "ASSUMED DETAILS" with a NYSDOT-style section (F2 / F9 / Type 2 subbase); raise it as an RFI/clarification. The geotech tables:
   - AC heavy duty: 1-1/4" SP9.5 + 1-3/4" SP19 + 8" GAB
   - AC light duty: 2" SP9.5 + 6" GAB
   - PCC heavy duty: 6-1/2" + 4" GAB
   - PCC light duty: 5" + 4" GAB
   - Subgrade: upper 12" at 98% Standard Proctor

   An app that reads the section from the estimate description would carry the wrong courses into any tonnage line.
3. **Two "21" legend rows with identical truncated names.** Teal 241.8 belongs to this trade (concrete paving). Purple 384.3 is the dumpster slab (trade 32b, line 663). Both rows are in legends.json; match them by colour and quantity, not by name.
4. **Two keynotes, one line.** Keynote 18 and keynote 21 have identical note text and the estimator merged them. Expect the estimate line to be the sum of two legend rows.
5. **Rounding is a ceiling.** 113.3 SF becomes 114 SF. Do not "fix" it to 113.
6. **Crosswalk and hatch are traced linework, not geometry.** 79.46 LF is not the 22.5' crossing. Store the traced LF as measured, and derive paint SF separately if needed. The crosswalk trace is 0.935 of its CAD geometry (79.46 / 85.0). The aisle hatch has no clean geometric match (CAD hatch 66.06 LF; hatch + sides 102.06, hatch + perimeter 112.06), so do not assume a common short-trace factor.
7. **Arrow size.** The detail is drawn at road size and says to halve it for parking lots. The estimator copied the road size (3'-8" x 9'-4").
8. **Sign code typo.** The legend and estimate say "RF-1" for DO NOT ENTER; the keynote says R5-1.
9. **Bike rack detail missing.** Keynote 19 says "SEE SHEET C-6.0", but there is no rack detail on C-6.0. The pipe, the legs per rack and the footing size are all assumptions. Flag them for an RFI.
10. **Two conflicting bollard details.** Civil C-6.0 BOLLARD DETAIL: 24" Ø footing, "4' MINIMUM" embedment from the pavement surface to the pipe bottom with 3" of concrete below the pipe (4'-3"), "3' MINIMUM" exposed above the pavement, 6" x 7' pipe. The architectural details call for 16" piers. The estimator used the architectural details for both groups. With the civil footing, the 5 dumpster bollards alone would be 2.327 CY (4'-0") or 2.473 CY (4'-3") instead of 0.772 CY.
11. **The legend hides part of the plan** (the Westgate Dr entrance, stop bar and arrows). Read those areas on the clean sheet.
12. **Existing curb labels.** The grey "24" ROLL CURB" and "24"C&G" text on the public streets is survey linework, not new scope.
13. **Pier depths are not all on the drawings.** A-190 gives no depth for the 16" dumpster pier (the estimate's 3'-0" is an assumption), and A-401's 48" is the pipe embedment, with 3" more fill below the pipe (51"). Record which depth is drawn and which is assumed.
